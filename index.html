What it does

INLINE

Works in chats it isn't even in

Type @AartiMusic_bot plus a song name in any conversation. Results appear as you type, and the track sends straight into the chat.

VOICE

Say the song instead of typing it

Send a voice note in a DM — or reply /vs to one in a group. Speech gets transcribed, cleaned up, and searched. Hinglish and half-remembered titles are fine.

CALLS

Plays into group voice chats

/vplay starts a track in the call. Queue up more, skip, pause, and let it roll — the next song downloads in the background before the current one ends.

VAULT

Keeps the ones you liked

Tap the heart under any track to save it. /myfavorites brings the collection back, and /favshuffle plays the whole thing on shuffle.

SPEED

Popular tracks arrive instantly

Anything downloaded once is stored and served from Telegram's own servers. Common songs skip the download entirely and send in about a second.

TASTE

Suggests where to go next

The suggest button reads the track you're on and what you've saved, then offers four songs that fit the same mood, language and era.
