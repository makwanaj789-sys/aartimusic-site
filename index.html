02 — What it does

FEATURES

Everything runs inside Telegram. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

01

Works in chats it isn't even in

Type @AartiMusic_bot plus a song name in any conversation. Results appear as you type, and the track sends straight into the chat.

02

Seven effects, one tap

Slowed, reverb, slowed + reverb, 8D, concert hall, bass boost, nightcore. Pick a default in settings and every track arrives that way.

03

Make any track your own

Change the cover, the title, the artist. Effects and edits stack, and your copy stays yours — the original is untouched for everyone else.

04

Say the song instead of typing it

Send a voice note, or reply /vs to one in a group. Speech gets transcribed, cleaned up, and searched. Hinglish and half-remembered titles are fine.

05

Plays into group voice chats

/vplay starts a track in the call. Queue more, skip, pause — the next song downloads in the background before the current one ends.

06

Popular tracks arrive instantly

Anything downloaded once is served from Telegram's own servers. Common songs skip the download entirely and send in about a second.

04 — Reference

COMMANDS

In a DM you can skip the command and just send a song name.

/vsReply to a voice note to search it
/vplay <song>Play in the group voice chat
/favshuffleShuffle your saved tracks
/vqueueSee what's lined up next
/myfavoritesOpen your vault
/mystatsYour listening numbers
/setChoose who can control playback
